## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. 📊 Smart Trimester & Semester GPA Calculator
- Designed strictly following **United International University (UIU)** grading policies.
- Calculate term GPA, cumulative CGPA, and degree completion status in real-time.
- Interactive course addition with course code, title, credit hours, and letter grades.

### 2. 🎯 Quick GPA Target Predictor
- Predicts the exact required future GPA per term to hit your dream CGPA.
- Dynamic difficulty assessment badges:
  - 🟢 **Easily Attainable** (≤ 3.30)
  - 🟡 **Challenging** (3.31 – 3.75)
  - 🟠 **Extremely Demanding** (3.76 – 4.00)
  - 🔴 **Math Impossibility / Unreachable** (> 4.00)

### 3. 💳 Official Tuition Fee Breakdown & PDF Generator
- Full support for both **Trimester** and **Semester** systems.
- Configurable per-credit fees and trimester/semester session fees with auto-save.
- **Scholarship vs Waiver Policy Handling**:
  - UIU Scholarship discount is strictly capped at **13 credits**.
  - Waivers apply across all registered credits.
- **Retake Discount Automation**:
  - **1st-time Retake**: Automatic **50% course fee discount** as per UIU policy.
  - **Subsequent Retakes**: Full tuition with applicable scholarship/waiver deductions.
- **Fine & Installment Scheduler**:
  - Calculates 1st, 2nd, and 3rd installment due amounts and late fine deductions.
- **Executive PDF Export**:
  - Generates official, pixel-perfect, two-column PDF receipts ready for printing, archiving, or sharing.

### 4. 📑 Transcript Management & Retake Intelligence
- Import academic records from PDF transcripts or JSON backups.
- **Smart Retake Deduplication**: Retaken courses retain the best achieved grade towards cumulative CGPA.
- **Withdrawn (W) & Incomplete (I) Policy**:
  - `W` (Withdrawn) grades are completely excluded from CGPA calculations.
  - `I` (Incomplete) grades count as 0.00 until cleared.
- **Smooth Swipe-to-Delete**:
  - Swipe any trimester card left to remove the whole trimester.
  - Swipe individual course cards left with a confirmation safeguard to remove specific courses.

### 5. 🏅 UIU Convocation Honors & Distinction Tracker
- Evaluates academic standing against UIU's official honors thresholds:
  - 🥇 **Chancellor's Gold Medal**: CGPA ≥ 3.90 (undergraduate/graduate)
  - 🎖️ **Summa Cum Laude**: CGPA ≥ 3.90
  - 🥈 **Magna Cum Laude**: CGPA 3.80 – 3.89
  - 🥉 **Cum Laude**: CGPA 3.75 – 3.79
- Dynamically shows unlocked achievements and remaining requirements.

### 6. 🔒 100% Offline & Private First
- **Zero remote telemetry**: No student name, student ID, CGPA, or marks are uploaded to external servers.
- All records, settings, and calculations are stored **100% locally** in device storage using encrypted preferences.
- One-click **JSON Backup & Restore** for easy device-to-device migration.

### 7. 🤖 AI Academic Advisor
- Powered by Cloudflare Workers AI with fallback engine.
- Provides personalized study advice, prerequisite sequencing, and workload balancing recommendations.
