Summary of UIU Rules
Official Tuition Fee & Payment Policies
Scholarship vs. Tuition Waiver
• Scholarship: Discount applies to regular & subsequent retakes up to a maximum of 13 credits.
• Tuition Waiver: Has NO credit limit and applies to all registered eligible credits.
First-Time Retake (50% Flat Discount)
Every student automatically receives a 50% discount on that course's tuition fee for 1st-time retakes, regardless of scholarship or waiver.
Subsequent Retakes & Scholarship/Waiver
Subsequent retakes (2nd time or more) do not get the automatic 50% discount, but ARE eligible for Scholarship or Tuition Waiver discounts!
Trimester / Semester Session Fee
Fixed academic session fee is NOT subject to any discount or waiver and is always added as-is.
Installment Schedules
• Trimester Students (3 Installments): 40% (1st) → 30% (2nd) → 30% (3rd)
• Semester Students (4 Installments): 25% → 25% → 25% → 25%
Payment Failure Policy
If a student misses an installment payment deadline, a late fine of 500 BDT per missed installment is strictly added.
